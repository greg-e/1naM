# Operational Dossier: Single-Pilot Transatlantic Emergency Diversion & Autonomous Landing

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Flight Profile

This document outlines the flight profile, crew rest structure, single-pilot procedures, and autonomous arrival parameters for an emergency transatlantic diversion involving an **Airbus A330-900neo**. 

The operational scenario assumes a complete disruption of ground infrastructure, rendering the destination airport an uncontrolled, non-towered field with no active Air Traffic Control (ATC) or ground support personnel.

### Flight Specifications
* **Flight Identification:** Delta Air Lines DL80
* **Routing:** Atlanta (ATL) $\rightarrow$ Frankfurt (FRA)
* **Diversion Airport:** St. John's International Airport (YYT / CYYT)
* **Aircraft Type:** Airbus A330-900neo
* **Powerplants:** 2 × Rolls-Royce Trent 7000-72 Turbofans
* **Maximum Takeoff Weight (MTOW):** 251 tonnes (553,360 lbs)
* **Passenger Capacity:** 281 Seats (29 Delta One, 28 Premium Select, 56 Comfort+, 168 Main Cabin)
* **Avionics Suite:** Dual FMS, Triple ADIRU/IRS, FLS (FMS Landing System), ROPS (Runway Overrun Prevention System), BTV (Brake-to-Vacate)

## 2. Flight Crew Structure & Rest Rotations

Under FAA Part 117 rules for long-haul transatlantic operations, this route utilizes a 3-pilot **augmented flight crew**. Every pilot holds an FAA Airline Transport Pilot (ATP) certificate with an identical **A330 Type Rating**, qualifying each to operate the aircraft independently in all phases of flight.

### Flight Deck Roster
1. **Captain (PIC):** Pilot-in-Command
2. **First Officer (SIC):** Senior First Officer
3. **Relief Pilot (SIC):** Fully qualified First Officer

### In-Flight Rest Schedule (Cruise Phase)

Overhead Flight Crew Rest (OFCR) bunks located above the forward cabin allow equal lie-flat rest periods while two pilots maintain the flight deck:

| Time Window (EDT) | Flight Deck Crew at Controls | Pilot in Overhead Rest Bunk | Operational Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **10:15 PM – 11:15 PM** | Captain + First Officer | *None* | Climbout / Departure Phase |
| **11:15 PM – 01:15 AM** | Captain + First Officer | Relief Pilot | Cruise Rest Shift 1 |
| **01:15 AM – 03:15 AM** | Captain + Relief Pilot | First Officer | Cruise Rest Shift 2 |
| **03:15 AM – 05:15 AM** | First Officer + Relief Pilot | Captain | Cruise Rest Shift 3 |
| **05:15 AM – Onward** | All 3 Pilots Active | *None* | Pre-Descent / Active Operations |

## 3. Emergency Timeline & Diversion Sequence

At **5:45 AM EDT**, approximately 500 nautical miles east-northeast of Newfoundland, a rare dual-pilot incapacitation occurs (e.g., severe environmental or toxicological issue affecting the overhead rest area and primary seats). The remaining active First Officer assumes single-pilot command.
