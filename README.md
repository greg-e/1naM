# 1 in a Million

A novel-in-progress (Book One of a planned series). The full draft, story bible, and production/continuity notes live in [1naM_Draft.md](1naM_Draft.md).

## Premise

A fast-acting biological event kills nearly everyone on Earth in a single night. Survival rate is roughly one in a million — around 350 people in the continental US, scattered with no concentration anywhere. Most survivors don't know they're immune until they wake up into the silence. The book follows the handful who find each other and start rebuilding some kind of shared life, without ever learning *why* they were the ones who lived.

## Status

This is a working draft under active revision. Large parts of the manuscript have been retconned (cut characters, cut plotlines, changed vehicles/aircraft) and not every chapter has been updated to match yet — see the "MAJOR PENDING RETCON" and "RETCON STATUS" notes inside the draft for exactly what's settled and what's still open.
