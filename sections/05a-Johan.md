# Johan Brandt

### 9/5/2028, 14:41 ship's time

The printer on the GMDSS console ran out a short length of paper and stopped.

Johan was at the chart table with the crew manifest, writing the names into the official log. He had brought Okafor down that morning in the Neil Robertson stretcher, in the elevator and along the main deck passage, and gone back for Alvarez and then for the cook. They lay in the chill room off the galley now, with the vegetables stacked out in the passage. The others he had left in their cabins with the doors shut. *Bautista, R., chief cook, Iloilo.* He finished the line before he got up.

The Inmarsat-C set had printed nothing all day, no gale warning, no ice report. He tore off the paper.

*ALL SHIPS ALL SHIPS ALL SHIPS. THIS IS JOHN HARTMAN, ALPHARETTA, GEORGIA, USA. THIS IS NOT AN OFFICIAL COAST GUARD BROADCAST. SINCE 0600 UTC 05 SEP THERE HAS BEEN NO RESPONSE FROM ANY AIR TRAFFIC CONTROL, COAST GUARD, OR EMERGENCY SERVICE IN NORTH AMERICA. TWO SURVIVORS HERE. IF YOU RECEIVE THIS, CALL +1 404 555 0127 BY SATELLITE PHONE, OR REPLY TO THIS TERMINAL. GIVE YOUR SHIP NAME AND POSITION.*

He read it standing. Safety messages came from a coordinator with a number, in a format he could have recited, and they did not come from a man with a name. 0600 UTC was the figure he had worked out that morning off the bridge log. He read *TWO SURVIVORS HERE* again and looked out the windows for a while, at the stacks of containers and the gray water past them.

He had been alongside at Savannah twice. He had never heard of Alpharetta.

The bridge telephone went out through the broadband dome, which had quit that morning. He pulled its breaker and put it back, and the dome hunted around and found the satellite and showed him a full signal, and the network light stayed red. He dialed anyway, double-oh, one, four zero four, five five five, zero one two seven, and got a fast tone. He dialed it again, slower, and got the same tone.

*Reply to this terminal.* The message gave no number for any terminal. The Inmarsat-C could send text to anyone it had a number for, so he looked up the rescue center his position fell under, Alameda, and typed it out.

*MV PACIFIC TENDER 50-31N 173-15W COURSE 075 SPEED 11.5. ONE PERSON ALIVE ON BOARD. 22 DEAD. BOUND STRAIT OF JUAN DE FUCA. REQUEST ANY INFORMATION. BRANDT, SECOND OFFICER.*

The land station took it and printed back a delivery number in under a minute. He stood by the set until the radar wanted looking at.

He wrote the receipt in the deck log with the time, wrote the telephone number on a card, and taped the card to the console above the handset. Then he went back to the manifest. At three he tried the number again, and at four.

At 20:41 the printer ran again, the same message, word for word. He taped it beside the card and dialed.
