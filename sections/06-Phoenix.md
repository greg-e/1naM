# Phoenix

The day was clear, no clouds and temps in the mid 70s.  They climbed to 28,000 feet, Sam continued the routine of the day before of trying to contact every station along the route. John climbed into the back and pulled out sandwich makings from the cooler. He got a couple bottled waters and the sandwiches and returned to the right seat on the flight deck.

"I could get used to your cooking" Sam smiled as he took a bite of the sandwich. 

"I've been thinking about food, with the power grid failure, all we'll have are dry and canned goods. I've never slaughtered a cow or even a chicken for that matter, you?" 

"Nope" Sam replied, "I've always enjoyed learning new things and right now I welcome anything that will keep my mind off whatever this is."

**13:50 EDT**

"Stopping for fuel in Amarillo, we'll start our descent in 8 min." Sam said.

Before landing at Amarillo Sam flew a low circle around the airport.  The main runway, runway 4/22, what a disaster, what was left of a large aircraft was scattered hundreds of yards. Fires were scattered around in the fields next to the airstrip.

"Runway 31 it is," Said Sam. "Looks like general aviation ramp over there anyway."

John called out the checklist and they flew a regular downwind pattern for runway 31, Sam pulled up the tower frequency and announced each leg.

"Amarillo, Pilatus Six One Two Papa Romeo short final 31."

**13:15 local Central Time**

They touched down and Sam kept the power up rolling all the way to the end of the runway.  The FBO was adjacent to runway 22.

John pointed to a fuel truck with 'JETA' stenciled on the side. They parked on the ramp near it and spooled down the turbine.

"I need a pit stop." John said.  "Me too and right behind you," said Sam.

Haven FBO was very nice, typical high end corporate place catering to jet-set CEOs. The doors were open and mercifully no one had been hanging out. No dead bodies to walk around or try to ignore.  

They found the keys to the fuel truck and working together got 2PR fueled.

**13:50 local Central Time**

No wind, they departed on runway 13 and climbed out south turning west toward Phoenix.

"Alright," Sam said, "Catherine may or may not still be at the hospital, I was thinking of making a low pass over the hospital and they making several circles around the airport, see if we can get her attention."  John shook his head in agreement. "How are we doing on time?"

Sam looked at the iPad, "Around 15:00 local time in Phoenix, if we're gonna pull it off we will need to get Catherine within 40 minutes and top off again, we may not make it to Port Angeles today, hopefully Port Angeles can hang in there."

John thought for a minute, "Catherine seems like all business, should be easy to get her on board, anyway, it must be miserable there without AC, I hope she didn't head out."

**14:50 local Mountain Time**

Maricopa County hospital was just north of Phoenix Skyharbor International Airport. Sam decended to 800 feet AGL and flew just to the south of the tallest building running full pitch, which made the turbine sound like thunder. He pulled up and made a slow turn to the south and flew south to north over the hospital, then turned and made a wide arc around the airport, keeping a flat pitch on the turbine, making as much noise as possible.  

"That should do it, if she's there she had to hear and see us, I'll make two more circuits and land on runway 25L over by that General Aviation FBO."

**15:15 local Mountain Time**

They rolled out again keeping power to the end of the runway where Cutter Aviation was.

2PR spooled down near a line of fuel trucks, next to a Gulf Stream IV. Dan was anxious to get out.  The heat felt like a brick wall when John opened the door. They stepped out into a beautiful albeit blistering Arizona afternoon.
