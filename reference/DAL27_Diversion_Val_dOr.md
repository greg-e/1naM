## 3. Operational Timeline: Incapacitation to Touchdown

### Phase 1: The Incapacitation Event (03:30 AM EDT / 07:30Z)
* **Position:** Over southern James Bay ($\sim 51^\circ 15'\text{N},\ 081^\circ 20'\text{W}$), FL360, 480 kts TAS.
* **Event:** Pilot A experiences sudden, severe medical collapse while seated in the Left Seat.
* **Immediate Cockpit Response:**
  * Pilot C confirms positive aircraft control on autopilot.
  * Pilot C toggles transponder to **`Squawk 7700`**.
  * Emergency flight deck call chime activated to overhead crew rest bunks; Pilots B and D summoned immediately.
  * Pilot C notifies Montreal Center (CZUL) of flight deck medical distress and requests a holding block at FL360.

### Phase 2: Assessment, Reorganization & Telemedicine (03:33 AM – 04:00 AM EDT)
* **03:33 AM:** Pilot B (Senior FO) assumes Acting Pilot-in-Command duties in the Left Seat; Pilot D takes the Right Seat (Monitoring/Comms).
* **03:35 AM:** Pilot C and purser move Pilot A clear of the primary flight controls to the flight deck observer jumpseat / forward galley area. 
* **03:38 AM:** Flight attendants assist a physician passenger on board with initial vitals; automated external defibrillator (AED) and on-board emergency medical kits (EMK) deployed. Oxygen administered.
* **03:42 AM:** Satellite communication link established with Delta Operations Control (OCC) and University of Pittsburgh Medical Center (UPMC / STAT-MD).
* **03:45 AM – 03:55 AM:** Aircraft holds in a wide racetrack orbit over northern Ontario at FL360. STAT-MD physicians evaluate telemetry and clinical indications from the flight deck, diagnosing an acute, life-threatening cardiovascular/cerebrovascular event.
* **03:57 AM:** STAT-MD and Delta Dispatch confirm immediate divert required: time-to-hospital is critical. Continuing along polar route or returning to Atlanta is eliminated due to transit duration.

### Phase 3: The Diversion Decision (04:00 AM EDT / 08:00Z)
* **Decision:** Divert to **Val-d'Or Airport (CYVO)**, Quebec.
* **Operational Basis:**
  * Located 220 nm southeast (bearing $\sim 150^\circ$).
  * Possesses Runway 18/36 ($10,000\text{ ft} \times 150\text{ ft}$), capable of supporting an emergency widebody A350-900 wheel-stop.
  * Closest viable $10,000\text{ ft}$ strip relative to the holding position.
  * *Centre hospitalier de Val-d'Or* trauma unit located less than 5 miles from airport threshold.

### Phase 4: Descent, Fuel Jettison & Approach (04:00 AM – 04:30 AM EDT)
* **04:00 AM:** Pilot B declares emergency diversion to Montreal Center: *"Mayday Mayday Mayday, Delta 27, diverting Val-d'Or."*
* **04:02 AM:** Montreal Center clears DAL27 direct `YVO` VOR, descent pilot's discretion to $10,000\text{ ft MSL}$.
* **04:03 AM – 04:18 AM:** High-speed descent initiated at $-3,500\text{ ft/min}$. Fuel jettison active; overwing dump valves open to discharge $\sim 45,000\text{ lbs}$ of Jet-A over unpopulated Abitibi forest, bringing aircraft below Maximum Landing Weight ($456,000\text{ lbs}$).
* **04:19 AM:** Fuel jettison secured. Passing $10,000\text{ ft MSL}$ at 250 KIAS. Val-d'Or Radio / Flight Service Station alerted. Municipal fire tenders and ambulance units mobilized to the CYVO main apron.
* **04:24 AM:** Aircraft intercepts the ILS Runway 18 localizer ($110.3\text{ MHz}$) at $3,000\text{ ft MSL}$ from the north. Flaps FULL, gear down.

### Phase 5: Landing & Medevac Hand-off (04:30 AM EDT / 08:30Z)
* **04:30 AM EDT (08:30Z):** **TOUCHDOWN: CYVO Runway 18.** Threshold crossing at 140 KIAS. Reverse thrust and autobrakes deployed; aircraft rolls out smoothly along the $10,000\text{ ft}$ runway.
* **04:34 AM EDT:** Aircraft vacates Runway 18 via Taxiway Bravo and taxis to the Apron II / Avjet FBO hardstand.
* **04:38 AM EDT:** Ground power connected; mobile airstairs positioned at Door 1L. Sûreté du Québec officers and Val-d'Or paramedic trauma crews board for stabilization and urgent transit to *Centre hospitalier de Val-d'Or*.
