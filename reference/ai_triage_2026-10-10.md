# John's Data Triage: "Timestamp Zero"

What John's model collects and what it can tell him, mapped onto the scenes. "The model" is John's company research model coupled to Gemini, running on his credentials. Don't confuse it with the **AI triage agent**, the 911 answering bot from Section 1.

**Rule from Section 1: collect first while the pipes are open, analyze later.** On Day 0 John saves; the deep look comes later (Section 5 with Sam, then Port Angeles, then the IRB). Everything in Section 1 stays inside his authorized access. The safety layer comes off exactly once, in Section 5, for the 911 logs.

## What is known on Day 0

1. Everything stopped at 2:00 a.m. Eastern. Nobody on the page has the exact millisecond until the timestamp evidence below is looked at.
2. Infrastructure is undamaged. Grid, networks, and machinery run until fuel, faults, or the grid cascade stop them.
3. John doesn't know the cause and doesn't speculate on the page (see memory: POV observes only).

## Objective 1: Scope and infrastructure decay (Sections 1, 5)

- **Flight tracking (FlightAware, ADS-B):** Delta 27's diversion, DAL27PDK, long-haul flights running out of fuel. On page.
- **AIS, DOT cameras, Cospas-Sarsat beacons:** on page (Section 5 archive vs. live).
- **EIA balancing-authority data and frequency:** the grid model (see `grid_2026-10-10.md`, `power-grid-shutdown-timelines.md`). On page.
- **Internet routing (BGP route collectors such as RouteViews) and data center loss:** a good addition for the ticking clock on the archive, showing which networks are dropping off. Not on page yet `[decide]`.

## Objective 2: Find survivors (Section 5)

- **911 call logs only.** See `survivor_location_probability_matrix.md` for why it's the only search, why it yields three leads, and why social media, WebSDR, and ISP monitoring aren't used.

## Objective 3: The cause (collected Day 0, read later)

Public, machine-generated, timestamped data that needs no human in the loop. John doesn't have to know what he's looking for; it lands in the archive because he pulled NOAA and science feeds wholesale.

- **Space weather (NOAA SWPC):** Kp index, X-ray flux. Result: normal. Rules out a solar flare or CME. `[verify feed names]`
- **NASA GCN notices and SNEWS alerts:** automatic gamma-ray burst and neutrino-burst notices stamped 06:00:00 UTC. The key evidence. See `cause_and_effect_2026-10-10.md` §5. **Needs a prose hook in Section 1 or 5** `[decide]`.
- **Seismic (USGS) and atmospheric (NOAA):** normal. Rules out impact, explosion, and a chemical release (no pressure wave, no plume).
- **The 911 autodialer spike:** crash-detection and fall-detection calls at 02:00–02:02 in every time zone. Already in the Section 5 pull, discarded by the filter.
- **Orbital elements (TLEs) for the ISS:** in the archive. With the source direction, they explain the crew's survival.

**Not used:** raw IceCube or Super-K detector logs. They aren't public in real time; the automated alerts are. Smartwatch health telemetry (SpO₂ and the like) sits on consumer platforms John has no authorization for, and the 911 autodialer spike gives the same timestamp.

## Objective 4: Reference data for later analysis

- Charts, maps, manuals, agency datasets, photos: on page (Section 1).
- Medical reference (toxicology, CDC/WHO baselines) and public genomic datasets: plausible as part of "everything else" and useful to Catherine and Marchetti later. **Genomic data must never confirm why survivors lived on the page** (`cause_and_effect_2026-10-10.md` §4).
