# More to Think About: Unattended World Hazards

Because the "ghost lineage" immunity is highly specific to a human mitochondrial enzyme, all non-human biological life on Earth is completely unaffected. This, combined with the reality of unattended infrastructure, introduces several massive secondary hazards.

## 1. The Feral Menace (Apex Predator Shift)
*   **The Reality:** There are roughly 90 million pet dogs in the United States, along with thousands of zoo animals. When humanity vanishes, millions of pets will eventually break out of homes and yards.
*   **The Threat:** Within a week, extreme hunger will override domestication. Starving dogs will form massive, feral packs, becoming the immediate apex predators in suburban and urban environments. Zoo animals (big cats, bears) will also breach enclosures and migrate. Unarmed survivors scavenging for food face a severe, active biological threat long before radiation becomes an issue.

## 2. Unattended Flame (Urban Firestorms)
*   **The Reality:** The event occurred at 2:00 AM EDT, meaning the grid remains active while humanity vanishes. Ovens were left on, space heaters were running, and vehicles crashed at high speeds.
*   **The Threat:** Without human fire departments to contain them, simple kitchen fires or vehicle fires will spread unchecked. Entire subdivisions and urban centers will burn down over the first few weeks, choking the sky with thick, toxic smoke and forcing survivors to navigate active fire zones.

## 3. The Splashdown Trap (ISS Return Complications)
*   **The Reality:** Manually calculating the retro-burn is only half the battle; the physical landing introduces a lethal paradox.
*   **The Threat (SpaceX Crew Dragon):** Designed for ocean splashdown. Without a naval recovery fleet to pull the capsule out of the water and open the hatch from the outside, the crew is trapped. Opening the hatch on the open ocean will flood and sink the capsule. 
*   **The Threat (Soyuz):** Designed for solid ground landings (typically the Kazakh Steppe). While they can exit the capsule, they will be stranded in the desolate Asian wilderness, thousands of miles from the AI Architect in Georgia, with no functional international aviation infrastructure to travel home.

## 4. Cryptographic and GPS Collapse
*   **The Reality:** The digital world requires constant human maintenance to remain "sane."
*   **The Threat (GPS Drift):** Satellites require constant ground-station uploads to correct for relativistic time dilation. Without human maintenance, GPS clocks will drift within weeks, rendering location data useless and scrambling any systems relying on GPS timing.
*   **The Threat (Certificate Expiration):** The secure internet relies on SSL/TLS certificates that expire and rotate. As these expire without human administrators to override or renew them, the AI Architect will rapidly find himself permanently locked out of surviving secure federal and enterprise databases, even if the physical servers still have power.
