# John's Infrastructure Model: "The Cascade Model"

"The Architect" is John; "the AI" is his model. Section 5 shows the grid model on the page; the other variables are available for later scenes.

## The Catalyst (Visual Confirmation)
Following the ingestion of abstract telemetry, the Architect manually accesses municipal DOT and enterprise security CCTV feeds. Visual confirmation of mass casualties and the absence of emergency services shifts the operational priority from "Investigation" to "Immediate Survival." 

## Objective 5: Predictive Modeling of Infrastructure Failure
*The Architect tasks the AI with a localized, predictive physics model. The AI must calculate exactly how many hours of electricity, internet, and water the Architect has remaining at their physical location.*

### Variable 1: The Macro-Grid Collapse (Electricity)
Automation holds the grid for hours, not days. Canon physics is in `power-grid-shutdown-timelines.md` and Section 5; this entry must match them.
*   **The Data:** EIA hourly balancing-authority numbers and the frequency readings under them (nominal 60.000 Hz in North America).
*   **The Model:** Load barely changes at 2:00 a.m., because the dead don't switch anything off; it's the fuel and operator side that fails. Governors and automatic generation control hold frequency through a silent window of ~2 hours. Then thermal plants trip one at a time (coal hoppers run out in 12–14 hours with nobody running the conveyors; gas units trip on unattended faults), and each loss pulls frequency **down**. At ~59.5 Hz under-frequency load shedding starts cutting customers loose, and the interconnection cascades within minutes. (Capacitor banks and shunt reactors manage voltage, not frequency, and play no part in the countdown.)
*   **The Output:** A window for regional lights-out. Eastern Interconnection ~midnight ± 2 h, actual ~00:50 at John's house.

### Variable 2: Data Center Attrition (The Internet)
When the grid dies, the internet will fracture. The Architect needs to know how long they can maintain their global dragnet.
*   **The Data:** The AI maps the geographic locations of the Tier 1 ISP nodes and enterprise data centers routing the Architect's connection. 
*   **The Model:** Generator permits and fuel-storage records (state air-quality permits, facility data) give a rough count of hours of diesel at each data center `[verify sources]`. Many sites hold 24–72 h at full load `[verify]`, but nobody refuels them and cooling faults go unattended.
*   **The Output:** A secondary window estimating the hour the backup generators run dry, the server racks overheat, and the Architect loses all access to the outside world.

### Variable 3: Municipal Utilities (Water & Gas)
The Architect must know how long their physical shelter remains habitable.
*   **The Data:** Water-utility and pipeline control systems (SCADA). **John has no authorization for these.** Reaching them would be a second unauthorized intrusion, and the safety layer comes off only once (Section 5). Use public utility data (tank capacities, pump-station locations), or leave this for Grace to work out on the ground.
*   **The Model:** It calculates the remaining volume in local gravity-fed water towers and compares it against the drop in pressure from automated systems left running (like broken pipes or automated sprinklers). 
*   **The Output:** An estimate of when the taps in the Architect's building will run dry and when the natural gas lines will depressurize.

### Variable 4: Environmental Hazards (The Threat Radius)
An unattended world is highly volatile. The AI must ensure the Architect is not in a blast zone.
*   **The Data:** The AI scrapes EPA databases for nearby chemical refineries, fertilizer plants, and nuclear reactors.
*   **The Model:** Nuclear plants SCRAM safely within hours; the danger is the spent fuel pools once backup diesel and batteries run out, ~10–21 days after the trip (Section 5's *Sept 16*; see `power-grid-shutdown-timelines.md`). Chemical refineries require complex, human-supervised shutdown procedures to bleed off pressure. The AI runs atmospheric plume models based on current weather data.
*   **The Output:** A map overlay detailing toxic out-gassing threat zones and secondary fire risks from unmanaged industrial facilities in the Architect's immediate vicinity.
