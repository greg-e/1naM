## Yohan's parallel Day 0 thread (Section 14) — not merged into the master clock below

Section 14 tells Yohan's own Day 0 discovery story, running on the same calendar day as Sections 1-2 (September 5, 2028) but aboard the *Pacific Tender* in the North Pacific, three days out of Seattle. It is intentionally **not** interleaved into the John+Sam master clock below — the two threads don't touch again until Section 15, when John spots Yohan's ship on AIS several days later (Day 3-6 for the main group). Ship's clocks ran nine hours behind Greenwich for the crossing, putting the event at just after 9:00 PM local the evening before Yohan wakes — still within the "someone's always asleep on a rotating watch" logic that lets a ship (unlike a school or office) plausibly have both on-duty and off-duty casualties regardless of the exact local hour. Yohan's own on-page time references stay soft (no watch is precisely timestamped) — consistent with "day-numbering is relative, not exact."
