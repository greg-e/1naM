# Timeline — 1 in a Million (Book One)

Master chronology of every existing section in [1naM_Draft.md](1naM_Draft.md), in manuscript order, with the day number exactly as written in that section's own header. This draft is mid-revision (see [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md)) — day numbers are known to be unreliable in places (the author has confirmed relative order matters more than exact day-count accuracy). Where this file's own read-through found a numbering problem, it's noted inline with ⚠ and rolled up in [Known day-numbering issues](#known-day-numbering-issues) below.

Section numbers skip around (17-18, 26-27, 32-38, 56, 58-62, 65-74 don't exist) because those sections were deleted in an earlier retcon pass — see CLAUDE.md and the draft's own Story Bible for what was cut (Brody, Ashley, Devon, Ray, Diane, Owen, Priya, Caleb, the C-17, the Kazakhstan mission, Pelican Cove). None of those appear below because none of them survive in the current draft.

## Cross-character convergence points

Moments where separate POV threads intersect — useful for checking that both sides of an encounter stay consistent when either is revised:

- **§1–3**: John spots Sam's diverted flight on FlightAware before they've ever spoken; §3 is the first phone contact.
- **§3, §5, §6**: John's first three confirmed contacts, all Day 0 — Sam, Catherine, Tyler.
- **§7–9**: Sam reroutes to collect Tyler, then flies both of them to John in Atlanta — first in-person meeting of the three.
- **§11**: John/Sam/Tyler reach Catherine in Tucson.
- **§12–13**: Group established at TruckHouse; Grace added.
- **§19–22**: Yohan's isolated thread (started §14, Day 0) rejoins the main group after the longest solo stretch of anyone found (16 days).
- **§21**: Sam's unpaid-off "C-130s near Portland" beat — flag before reusing; per CLAUDE.md the C-17/heavy-airlift thread was cut entirely, so this idea should either be paid off as something else or explicitly dropped.
- **§25→§29–30**: Sam's realization about the ISS crew leads directly to the JSC trip and first contact with Voss.
- **§41 / §43-47**: Elise's discovery (Day 19) leads to the Milan retrieval mission (Day 24).
- **§53 / §54-55**: AKULA is spotted, then becomes the vessel used for the Dragon recovery.
- **§30 vs §63-64**: Voss's Day 17 reveal that 4 of 9 crew are already dead (Soyuz) is paid off at the Day 33 Dragon recovery of the remaining 5.

## Known day-numbering issues

Flagged during this read-through — none of these need to block ongoing work per CLAUDE.md's "day-numbering is relative, not exact" rule, but they're worth fixing in a dedicated continuity pass since a few are large enough to actually confuse reading order:

1. **§12 "Day 1"** sits directly after §11 "Day 8" and depicts the group's arrival at TruckHouse immediately following the Tucson rescue — should almost certainly read Day 8 or 9, not Day 1. Likely a leftover header from before the Day-1→Day-8 Tucson-delay retcon.
2. **§29 "Day 17"** contains internal prose referring to "eighteen days" since the event — off by one from its own header.
3. **§43 "Day 20"**: Sam references "Malpensa" as a past event ("not after Malpensa") four sections/four days before the Malpensa crash-landing actually happens in §45 (Day 24). Either a forward-reference to cut, or the crash needs to be moved earlier.
4. **§49 "Day 22"** (return from Milan) is dated *earlier* than both the Milan departure (§44-47, Day 24) and the Houston return stopover (§48, Day 28) that precedes it in-story. This is the single largest day-numbering problem in the draft — §49 likely belongs around Day 28-29.
5. **§50 and §51, both "Day 23"** — departure from the lodge and arrival at Mayport carry the same day number despite §50's own text saying the convoy drive "took the better part of four days." Cascades from issue #4 above.
6. **§53 "Day 24"** (Tyler spots AKULA) appears in reading order after §52 "Day 25," out of sequence without being flagged as a flashback.

## Open threads / dropped plot points

- **§19 (Yohan, Day 6)**: Yohan plans to scout Seattle by small boat "tomorrow." Never followed up — by §21 (Day 16) he's still aboard the anchored ship with no mention of the scouting trip. Confirm intent (dropped on purpose, or needs a scene) before continuing his arc.
- **§21 (Sam, Day 16)**: the "C-130s near Portland" spark is planted but never paid off. Per CLAUDE.md, the story's eventual heavy-airlift/C-17 solution was cut entirely — this beat should be either repurposed or removed rather than left dangling.
- **Tyler / Voss connection**: §6 establishes Tyler's mother is an ISS astronaut; §30 introduces Commander Voss. The draft's Story Bible treats "Voss is Tyler's mother" as planted-but-unconfirmed — nothing in the sections above actually states it on-page. Don't resolve this without author direction (see CLAUDE.md).
- **§57 dialogue bug**: Grace is written replying to and agreeing with her own line of dialogue ("This is objectively the nicest place I've ever lived..." / "'Truly,' Grace agreed...") — looks like a second speaker's name was dropped in editing. Needs a name fix, not a plot fix.
